# Community code of conduct

Parallax welcomes focused, good-faith collaboration across different technical
and political perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Address the work and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Represent other positions accurately before criticizing them.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep issue reports specific, reproducible, and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Help keep participation accessible to people with different backgrounds and
  levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or encouraging targeted harassment.
- Turning repository channels into partisan campaigning or repetitive debate.
- Deliberately misleading citations, fabricated evidence, or impersonation.
- Public disclosure of security vulnerabilities or credentials.
- Spam, manipulation, or coordinated disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community and the
project.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use the relevant issue or GitHub reporting
tools. For sensitive safety or security concerns, follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and do not disclose details publicly.
